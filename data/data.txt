The solar system consists of the Sun and all celestial bodies that orbit it. This includes eight planets: Mercury, Venus, Earth, Mars, Jupiter, Saturn, Uranus, and Neptune. 

Earth is the third planet from the Sun and the only one known to support life. It has one natural satellite, the Moon.

Jupiter is the largest planet in the solar system. It has a very strong magnetic field and more than 75 moons. Its most famous moon is Europa, which is believed to have a subsurface ocean.

The Sun is a star at the center of the solar system. It provides the energy that sustains life on Earth. It is primarily composed of hydrogen and helium.

Mars is often called the Red Planet because of its reddish appearance. It has the tallest volcano in the solar system, Olympus Mons.

Saturn is known for its beautiful ring system. It is mostly made of gas and has dozens of moons, including Titan, which has a dense atmosphere.

The solar system is part of the Milky Way galaxy. Our galaxy contains billions of stars, many of which may have their own planetary systems.
